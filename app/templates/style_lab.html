feat/ht2 experiment

Five ways to make Holtsnider Tech feel less AI and more like Chris.

This is a comparison board, not the final homepage. It keeps the current site’s best pieces — background, transparency, animation-friendly sections, services, portfolio, Daily Flyer, about, contact, and the Start concept — while testing different voices and page rhythms.

Style 01

Field Notes / Chris Explains It

Feel: first-person, plainspoken, visibly human.

Less agency brochure, more working notebook: what I build, what I notice, what I can help fix, and where the project is going next.

This site is a workbench, not a brochure.
Style 02

Daily Flyer Inspired

Feel: structured, card-based, themed, and alive.

Borrow the best Daily Flyer lesson: small, purposeful cards can make a project feel approachable, useful, and repeatable.

The pattern matters more than one finished page.
Style 03

Technical Workshop

Feel: diagnostic, hands-on, builder energy.

Lean into troubleshooting, testing, reliability, small tools, cleanup passes, and practical engineering judgment.

Bring the weird problem. I’ll help take it apart.
Style 04

Small Business Tech Translator

Feel: warm, plain-English, client-friendly.

Explain services through recognizable problems: websites, spreadsheets, inventory, confusing software, repeated manual work, and unclear next steps.

You can explain the problem badly. That is allowed.
Style 05

Boston Practical / Local Operator

Feel: grounded, restrained, local, competent.

Less startup gloss, more accountable and direct; a subtle Boston/New England feel without forcing skyline clichés.

No theater. Just a clearer way through the problem.
Next step

Pick the strongest one or combine two.

My starting recommendation is a blend of Field Notes, Technical Workshop, and Daily Flyer Inspired: human voice, concrete troubleshooting credibility, and a reusable card structure for projects and services.
