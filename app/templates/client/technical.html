My background is in reliability, infrastructure, automation, and troubleshooting across the boundaries between systems.

Here is the engineering view. No intake flow required.

Reliability & incident response

Enterprise storage reliability, production-impacting failures, replication quality, data integrity, and long-term stability. The work starts with understanding the failure and its impact—not choosing a tool in advance.

Focus: incident investigation, reproduction, validation, and operational follow-through.

Infrastructure & realistic test labs

Rack-level systems, networking, VLANs, Fibre Channel, Linux, and VMware. Build environments that reproduce the relevant customer conditions so that a proposed change can be tested against the actual problem.

Focus: dependencies, configuration, realistic workloads, and pre-rollout testing.

Automation & custom software

Python, APIs, command-line tools, Terraform, and GitHub workflows. Turn repeat work into something that can be run, reviewed, and maintained, with attention to how it fails as well as how it works.

Focus: repeatable processes, useful interfaces, and clear ownership.

How I approach the work

Understand the system and its constraints. Reproduce or narrow the problem. Make the smallest useful change. Validate it, explain the tradeoffs, and leave enough context for the next person.
