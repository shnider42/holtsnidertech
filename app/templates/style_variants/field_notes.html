Margin noteThis site is a workbench, not a brochure. The rough edge is intentional.
01 / Start

Say the problem badly.

A screenshot, a broken spreadsheet, a weird workflow, or a vague idea is enough. The first job is making the situation easier to describe.

02 / Build

Make one useful thing.

Not a giant platform. Not a buzzword stack. A prototype, cleanup pass, project page, or small automation that proves the next step.

03 / Explain

Leave it clearer.

The handoff should make sense to the person who has to use it, maintain it, or decide whether it deserves more investment.
