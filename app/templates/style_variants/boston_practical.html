Choose a path

The method is simple on purpose.

Make the mess visible, separate the layers, then build the next useful thing.

Step 01

Make it visible.

Gather the symptoms, constraints, files, pages, workflows, or decisions causing friction.

Step 02

Separate the layers.

Sort what is content, process, tooling, data, design, communication, or engineering.

Step 03

Build the next thing.

Create the fix, prototype, cleanup path, explanation, or page that moves the work forward.

Bring the messy version.

A direct note about what is not working, what you are trying to build, or what feels confusing is enough. Perfect requirements are not required.
