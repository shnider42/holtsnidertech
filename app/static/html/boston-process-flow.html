Problem solving + opportunity design

A useful solution starts before the tool choice. I look at who is affected, what is actually happening, where the friction lives, why it matters, and what constraints shape the answer.

Messy input

Bug, issue, feature idea, repeated friction, unclear process, or product gap.

When something is breaking

Fix what is failing

  • Diagnose the issue
  • Find the affected people or process
  • Identify the failing layer
  • Stabilize the path forward
When something could be better

Shape the opportunity

  • Map the current process
  • Find repeated friction
  • Identify the leverage point
  • Shape a feature, workflow, or tool
Who

Who has the pain, who decides, and who has to live with the result?

What

What is happening, being requested, or getting in the way?

When

What timeline, rhythm, urgency, or trigger matters?

Where

Does the friction live internally, externally, or across both?

Why

What are the stakes, for whom, and what does inaction cost?

How

What constraints shape the path: budget, access, risk, people, or tooling?

The solution is not assumed

It is found

Sometimes the answer is a fix. Sometimes it is a clearer process, a better workflow, a practical prototype, or a feature direction that makes the product more useful.
